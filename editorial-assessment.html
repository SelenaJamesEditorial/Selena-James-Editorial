Service 02

Editorial Assessment

A comprehensive evaluation of where the manuscript stands now and where focused revision can have the greatest impact.

What this service does

I assess the manuscript as a whole and deliver a detailed letter explaining what is working, where opportunities exist, and which revisions are most likely to strengthen the project.

What you receive

  • A full-manuscript reading
  • A detailed assessment of structure, character, pacing, stakes, voice, and overall narrative effectiveness
  • Prioritized recommendations for revision
  • Professional perspective on possible next steps

What is not included

This service does not include comments or edits within the manuscript itself. It is designed to provide direction and perspective rather than scene-by-scene guidance.

When it is useful

An assessment can help when you have revised independently but are uncertain what still needs work, when you want an experienced perspective before querying, or when you need to decide whether a full developmental edit is the right next investment.

Best for

Writers seeking an expert overview before deciding whether to revise further, query agents, submit to publishers, self-publish, or pursue a more intensive edit.

Before booking

Send your genre, word count, project description, stage of revision, goals, and links to sample pages and a synopsis or outline.
