Service 03

Manuscript Strategy Session

A focused one-hour conversation about a manuscript challenge, revision plan, submission question, or publishing decision.

How the session works

You send your questions and relevant background in advance. During the session, we focus on the decisions that are most important to you and work through the available options together.

Possible topics

  • Structural or character decisions
  • Revision priorities
  • Whether the manuscript is ready for outside feedback
  • Querying or submission strategy
  • Positioning, genre, or readership
  • Which editorial service makes sense

What the session includes

The session provides direct access to experienced editorial guidance. It does not include a full manuscript read or a written editorial assessment unless those services are arranged separately.

How to prepare

Bring a concise description of the project, the decisions you are weighing, and the questions you most want answered. The more specific the questions, the more focused and useful the conversation can be.

Best for

Writers who have specific questions and want experienced editorial insight without committing to a manuscript-wide service.

Before booking

Send your genre, word count, project description, stage of revision, goals, and links to sample pages and a synopsis or outline.
