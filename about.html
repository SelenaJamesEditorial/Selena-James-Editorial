About

Editorial judgment shaped by more than twenty-five years in publishing.

I have evaluated manuscripts, acquired books, coached authors, and helped shape publishing programs at Simon & Schuster, Kensington, and Amazon Publishing.

The work

I have worked with writers ranging from debut novelists to bestselling authors, including Fredrik Backman, Elin Hilderbrand, Sally Hepworth, Joe Hill, Rebecca Serle, Katherine Center, Lisa Wingate, and Christina Baker Kline.

Across thousands of manuscripts, I have learned that revision is often a process of discovery. The most useful feedback does more than identify problems. It helps the writer see what the manuscript is already trying to become and determine which changes will bring that intention into sharper focus.

Whether you are preparing to query agents, submit to a publisher, or publish independently, my role is to help you understand what is working, what is getting in the way, and where revision will make the greatest difference.

What I look for

Is the story structure supporting the narrative?

Are the characters earning their emotional moments?

Is the pacing creating momentum?

Are the stakes clear and compelling?

Which revisions will make the greatest difference?

My approach

Specific, practical, manuscript-led.

01

Understand the writer’s goals

I begin with what you are trying to accomplish, not a generic prescription for how a book should work.

02

Preserve the writer’s voice

I do not rewrite the book or impose my vision. The feedback is designed to strengthen your choices.

03

Prioritize impact

Not every issue deserves equal attention. I identify the revisions most likely to improve the manuscript as a whole.

04

Make revision actionable

Strong editorial feedback explains both what is not working and why, so you can make informed decisions on the page.
