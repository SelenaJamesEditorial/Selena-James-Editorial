Editorial philosophy

Feedback should serve the book you are trying to write.

I begin by understanding your goals, then identify the changes most likely to support them. I do not rewrite books or impose my vision. I help authors see their manuscripts more clearly and make informed revision decisions.

What happens next

A clear, low-friction inquiry process.

  1. 1

    Send your inquiry

    Share your genre, word count, project description, goals, and sample-page link.

  2. 2

    I review the fit

    I consider the manuscript, your goals, and the level of editorial support that would be most useful.

  3. 3

    You receive a recommendation

    I will let you know whether I am the right editor for the project and which service I recommend.

  4. 4

    We schedule the work

    If we decide to move forward, I will confirm scope, timing, deliverables, and fee before the project begins.
